# Center for High Throughput Computing (CHTC)

## Overview

The Center for High Throughput Computing provides advanced computing resources and expertise to support research and scientific discovery.

## Responsibilities

The bulk of my work in this rotation is provisioning and decommissioning hosts. That work includes upgrading nodes to AlmaLinux 10, including nodes in Kubernetes clusters. I also support Linux-based research computing infrastructure, including HTCondor operations and troubleshooting, Ceph storage and data management workflows, and configuration and change management processes using Puppet and Python.

I also do hardware work when troubleshooting requires it. That includes hardware troubleshooting for GPU dropouts and investigating Ceph storage dropouts, alongside routine infrastructure maintenance and system administration in large-scale computing environments.
