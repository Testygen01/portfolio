# DoIT General Help Desk

## Overview

The DoIT General Help Desk is the front line of information technology support for students, faculty, staff, and affiliates at the University of Wisconsin-Madison.

## Responsibilities

I provided Tier 1 and Tier 2 technical support across account management, authentication, endpoint support, and software and hardware troubleshooting. My work included service account requests under identity and access management principles, Duo multi-factor authentication, and FIDO2 authentication. I supported endpoint devices running Windows, macOS, and ChromeOS. I escalated complex cases to specialized support teams, and I documented troubleshooting findings and resolutions for each case.
