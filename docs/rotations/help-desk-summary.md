# DoIT General Help Desk

## Overview

**Duration:** First Rotation (6 Months)

The DoIT General Help Desk serves as the front line of information technology support for students, faculty, staff, and affiliates at the University of Wisconsin-Madison.

During this rotation, I provided technical support across a wide range of technologies and services, including account management, authentication, endpoint support, and software troubleshooting. This experience established my foundation in structured troubleshooting, customer communication, and incident management.

## Key Responsibilities

- Provided Tier 1 and Tier 2 technical support
- Troubleshot software and hardware issues
- Assisted users with authentication and access problems
- Supported endpoint devices including Windows, macOS, and chromeOS systems
- Escalated complex issues to specialized support teams
- Documented troubleshooting findings and resolutions

## Major Accomplishments

- Resolved Python environment and dependency issues affecting academic coursework
- Diagnosed authentication and browsser compatibility issues involving DUO MFA and FIDO2 authentication
- Assisted with service account requests while applying identity and access management principles
- Investigated and remediated ChromOS device issues involving cryptohome corruption and Linux environment failures
- Developed a systematic approach to troubleshooting end-user technology problems
