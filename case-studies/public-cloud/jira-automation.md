# Automating Jira Workflows to Improve Team Operations

## Overview

Operational ticket management often involves repetitive administrative tasks that consume time and introduce opportunities for inconsistency. To improve efficiency, I designed and implemented several Jira automations to streamline team workflows.

## Environment

- Jira Cloud
- Agile Operations
- Team Service Management Processes
- Regular Expressions (Regex)

## Problem

The team relied on manual actions to manage portions of the ticket lifecycle which created administrative overhead and reduced time available for technical work.

## Actions Taken

- Analyzed the Cloud team ticket workflow
- Identified automation opportunities
- Designed 7 custom Jira automation rules
- Implemented all seven automations
- Tested the automation behavior and expected outcome

## Outcome

The automation rules were successfully integrated into the team's workflow and significantly reduced the need for routine manual ticket management activities. One of the more visible outcome was the cleanup of over 500+ ticket alert ticket queue clogging up space.

## Key Takeaways

This project showed me the benenfits of automating routine taks that can be time consuming and energy draining. Automation is a force multiplier, and even small improvements can significantly improve operational efficiency.
